Check a prospectus yourself

Pick the official prospectus you have. This page reads it on your phone or computer. The file is never uploaded. You get a small file with the course numbers it found, plus your own notes. Sending that file helps us compare against what our scripts read.

Nothing you send is published. Two reviewers must check a row against the official document before it is called confirmed. No names, phone numbers, emails or ID numbers are included.
