# Mzansi Pathways v12 — Accessibility, Family & Keyboard Guide

- **Senior & Easy View (`Easy` button):** Enlarges base text (`118%`), enforces 46px+ button targets, and keeps controls calm.
- **Parent / Guardian View (`👨‍👩‍👧 Parent`):** Highlights cost, distance from home, legitimacy registers, and Plan A/B/C side-by-side.
- **Read Aloud (`🔊 Read Aloud`):** Uses South African English / Afrikaans browser speech synthesis to read NSC pass level and APS.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts:** `Alt+1` (Results), `Alt+2` (Careers), `Alt+3` (All Routes), `Alt+4` (Plan A/B/C), `Alt+5` (Evidence & CV), `Alt+6` (Work & Scams), `Esc` (Close modals).
