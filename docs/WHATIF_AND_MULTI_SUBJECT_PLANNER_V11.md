# Mzansi Pathways v15 — Multi-Subject What-If Planner & Accessibility Stack

- **Multi-Subject What-If Simulator (`#whatif-sub-select`):** Grade 11 and rewrite learners can pick any non-LO subject (Mathematics, Physical Sciences, English HL, IT, etc.) and simulate improving their mark while keeping real marks untouched.
- **Full Accessibility Stack:** Independent `A+` (Large Text), `Easy` (46px+ targets), `HC` (High Contrast), `🌙` (Dark Mode), `🔊` (Read Aloud), and `Alt+1`–`Alt+6` keyboard shortcuts.
- **32-Gate Verification:** Gate 32 (`32_verify_docs_completeness.py`) enforces that every specification and runbook file is present and free of incomplete placeholders.
